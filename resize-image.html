About Image Resizer

Need to resize an image for social media, a website, or a specific pixel size? This tool uses Lanczos3 resampling (via the pica library) for the highest-quality downscaling and upscaling — producing sharp, clean results without the blurriness or aliasing artifacts that basic resizing methods create. Choose from popular presets — Instagram Post (1080x1080), Instagram Story (1080x1920), YouTube Thumbnail (1280x720), Twitter Header (1500x500), Facebook Cover (820x312), LinkedIn Banner (1584x396), and more — or enter custom dimensions with an optional aspect ratio lock to prevent distortion. Whether you are preparing product images for an online store, creating consistently sized thumbnails for a blog, or formatting photos to meet a specific platform's requirements, this tool handles it in seconds. The aspect ratio lock ensures your images are never stretched or squished. Everything runs in your browser with no upload required, so your images remain completely private. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, and GIF input formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lanczos3 resampling?

Lanczos3 is a high-quality image resampling algorithm that produces sharp, artifact-free results when scaling images up or down. It's the same algorithm used by professional image editors like Photoshop.

Will resizing make my image blurry?

Downscaling (making smaller) always looks great with Lanczos3 resampling. Upscaling (making larger) preserves detail well but cannot add information that isn't in the original — very large upscales may appear slightly soft.

What dimensions should I use for social media?

Use the preset buttons: Instagram Post is 1080×1080, Instagram Story is 1080×1920, Twitter Header is 1500×500, LinkedIn Banner is 1584×396, YouTube Thumbnail is 1280×720.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Transmute processes everything locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device — there is no server, no upload, no cloud processing.
