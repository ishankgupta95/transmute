About Lorem Ipsum Generator

Lorem Ipsum is the standard placeholder text used in design, typography, and web development since the 1960s. When building website mockups, designing print layouts, or prototyping user interfaces, you need realistic-looking text that does not distract reviewers with actual content. Lorem Ipsum fills that role perfectly — it has a natural distribution of letters and word lengths that mimics real English text. This tool generates Lorem Ipsum in three formats: paragraphs (ideal for filling page layouts and article mockups), sentences (useful for shorter text blocks and card components), and individual words (perfect for labels, headings, and form placeholders). Choose the count you need and the text is generated instantly. The output always begins with the classic 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet...' opening for authenticity. Copy to your clipboard with one click and paste directly into your design tool, HTML file, or content management system. Whether you are a graphic designer mocking up a brochure, a web developer building a prototype, or a product manager creating wireframes, this tool saves you from typing gibberish.

Questions

Is my data sent to a server?+

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data leaves your device.

What is Lorem Ipsum?+

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used in design and typesetting since the 1500s. It has a natural letter distribution similar to English, making layouts look realistic before final copy is written.

Can I generate paragraphs, sentences, or words?+

Yes. You can choose to generate a specific number of paragraphs, sentences, or words depending on your layout needs.

Is the text random each time?+

The text is shuffled from a pool of classical Lorem Ipsum phrases, so each generation produces a slightly different arrangement while maintaining natural-looking Latin text.

Is my file uploaded to a server?+

No. Transmute processes everything locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device — there is no server, no upload, no cloud processing.
