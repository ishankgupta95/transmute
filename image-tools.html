Compress JPG, PNG, and WebP images by quality or to a target file size in KB. Smart binary search picks the best quality automatically.

Compress Image to 200 KB

Compress any image to under 200 KB — ideal for web uploads, document attachments, and platforms that cap file size at 200 KB.

Compress Image to 1 MB

Compress any image to under 1 MB — great for social media uploads, messaging apps, and email attachments with reasonable size caps.

Image Resizer

Resize images to exact dimensions or social media presets (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn). Lanczos3 resampling preserves sharpness.

Image Cropper

Crop images visually with interactive handles, aspect ratio presets, and rotation. Export as PNG with full quality preserved.

EXIF Data Viewer & Remover

Inspect and strip EXIF metadata from photos — camera model, GPS coordinates, timestamps. Protect privacy before sharing online.

Convert images to Base64-encoded data URIs for embedding directly in HTML, CSS, JSON, or email. One-click copy to clipboard.

PNG to ICO

Convert PNG images to ICO format for website favicons. Generates multi-size ICO files (16, 32, 48 px) compatible with every browser.

Favicon Generator

Generate a complete favicon set from one image — ICO, Apple Touch, Android, and PWA manifest icons. Download as a ready-to-deploy ZIP.

Image Color Palette

Extract the dominant colors from any image using median-cut quantization. Get hex codes and download a palette for design or branding work.

Format Converters

Convert iPhone HEIC and HEIF photos to JPG instantly in your browser. Batch-process multiple files with adjustable quality. No upload, no sign-up.

WebP to JPG

Convert WebP images to JPG instantly for use in apps, email clients, or older software that doesn't support WebP. Adjustable quality, no uploads.

WebP to PNG

PNG to JPG

Convert PNG images to JPG with adjustable quality to dramatically reduce file size. Perfect for email, web uploads, and social media.

JPG to PNG

Convert JPG to PNG for lossless quality and to add transparency support. Ideal before further editing or for design workflows.

JPG to WebP

PNG to WebP

SVG to PNG

AVIF to JPG
