This tool transcodes your WebM file to H.264/AAC MP4 — the most widely supported video format across virtually every device, platform, and application. In-browser encoding requires Chrome or Edge. This makes it safe for converting screen recordings of confidential work, product demos, internal presentations, and any other sensitive content.
