Fixing that means rewriting one field, not re-encoding a single frame, and the difference is not subtle: on a ten-second 720p clip the flag rewrite measured six milliseconds against roughly seven and a half seconds for the equivalent re-encode, and the flag rewrite is lossless while the re-encode is not.
