Convert PDF to JPG — Free Online Tool

Render each page of your PDF as a high-quality JPG or PNG image.

How It Works

  1. 1Upload your PDF by dragging it into the drop zone or clicking to browse.
  2. 2Adjust the JPG quality and select which pages to convert (optional).
  3. 3Click Download to save the resulting images.

About PDF to JPG

Need images from a PDF? Whether you want to turn a presentation into individual slide images, extract pages from a brochure for social media, convert a scanned document into image files, or create thumbnails of a multi-page report, this tool renders every page of your PDF to a high-quality image using PDF.js. Choose between JPG output (smaller file sizes, ideal for photos and general use) and PNG output (lossless quality, perfect for text-heavy documents and graphics with sharp edges). Adjust the quality slider and scale factor to control the resolution of the output — increase the scale for crisp, high-DPI images suitable for print or retina displays. Download individual pages one at a time, or download all pages at once for convenience. The rendering engine faithfully reproduces text, vector graphics, embedded images, and complex layouts from the original PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What resolution are the output images?

By default, pages are rendered at 2× scale for crisp output. You can adjust the scale to get higher or lower resolution images.

Can I choose PNG instead of JPG?

Yes. Toggle between JPG and PNG output. PNG is lossless and larger; JPG is smaller with adjustable quality.

How do I convert just one page?

Use the page selector to pick the page or page range you want — for example, 5 or 3-7. Only those pages are rendered to images, and the result downloads as a single image or a small ZIP.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Transmute processes everything locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device — there is no server, no upload, no cloud processing.
