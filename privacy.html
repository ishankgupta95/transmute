Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Overview

Transmute is designed to be private by default. All file processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly and native browser APIs. Your files are never uploaded to any server operated by Transmute or any third party.

Files You Process

When you use any Transmute tool, the files you select stay on your device. They are read into your browser’s memory, processed locally, and the result is offered as a download. At no point are your files transmitted over the network. Once you close the tab, the browser releases all associated memory.

Cookies & Local Storage

Transmute does not set any first-party cookies or store personal data in local storage. If third-party advertising is enabled on the site, ad network scripts may set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Analytics

Transmute may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns such as page views and popular tools. No personally identifiable information is collected, and no data about the files you process is ever recorded.

Third-Party Advertising

Transmute may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

External Resources

Some tools load open-source WebAssembly modules from CDNs (for example, FFmpeg.wasm from unpkg). These requests are standard browser resource fetches and do not transmit any of your file data.

Children’s Privacy

Transmute does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The site is a general-purpose utility tool and does not require account creation or personal data entry.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the maintainer at ishank.dev.
