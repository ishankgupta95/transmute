3c:T7f8,A watermark is how a PDF says what it is before anyone reads a word of it: DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, a client's logo, a copy number. This tool puts one on your pages without uploading anything. Drop a PDF, choose a text mark or a PNG or JPEG image, and place it on the rendered first page — drag the ring where you want it, or type exact percentages across and down, or move it with the arrow keys. The two number fields and the drag are the same pair of numbers, so a keyboard reaches every position a mouse does. Position is a share of each page rather than a fixed measurement, so the mark lands in the same relative spot on a document whose pages are not all one size, and it is measured against the page as your reader sees it: a cropped page, a page whose box does not start at zero, and a page the reader turns ninety degrees are all handled. Size, colour, boldness, opacity and rotation are yours; tick tile and the mark repeats twelve times on a fixed grid, which is the classic diagonal look, and the anchor is then ignored. Tick place under the page content and your text and pictures print over the top of it instead. Stamp every page, only the first, only the last, or a range like 1-3, 5, 8-10.
