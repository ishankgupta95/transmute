BUNDLED, NOT SERVED — a handful of tools reach for a parsing library that arrives inside that tool’s own code-split chunk instead of as a file named above. It is a real download and this is roughly the size of it: about 925 KB of bwip-js on /barcode-generator; about 566 KB of svgo on /svg-optimizer; about 478 KB of mammoth on /docx-to-markdown; about 288 KB of sql-formatter on /sql-formatter; about 124 KB of jszip on /create-zip and /unzip-files. Smaller ones — utif2 for TIFF, pica for resizing, js-yaml, marked, turndown, diff, qrcode, gifenc, and the JavaScript loaders for the WebAssembly engines above — are at most tens of kilobytes each rather than hundreds; oxipng’s wasm-bindgen loader, the smallest of them, is 6,307 bytes. Every figure here says “about” because the bundler decides how a library is split, and the same library measures differently on two pages; the numbers come from this site’s own static export.
