About M4A to MP3

M4A is Apple's preferred audio format — used by iTunes, Apple Music, iPhone voice memos, and GarageBand exports. While M4A (AAC) offers better compression than MP3 at the same bitrate, MP3 remains the most universally compatible audio format, supported by virtually every device, media player, and platform in existence. This tool bridges that compatibility gap by decoding M4A files using your browser's built-in AAC decoder (via the Web Audio API) and re-encoding to MP3 with the lamejs encoder. No plugins, no uploads, and no server processing — everything happens locally in your browser. Choose your preferred bitrate from 128 kbps (smaller files, suitable for voice recordings) to 320 kbps (highest quality, ideal for music). Common use cases include converting iPhone voice memos for sharing with Android users, preparing iTunes music files for older MP3 players, converting podcast recordings for universal distribution, and making GarageBand exports compatible with platforms that only accept MP3. Since the conversion runs entirely on your device, your audio files remain completely private — important for personal voice memos, confidential recordings, and licensed music content. No account required and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is M4A?

M4A is an audio container format used by Apple. It typically contains AAC (Advanced Audio Coding) data. iPhones use M4A for voice memos, and iTunes/Apple Music downloads are M4A files.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Transmute processes everything locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device — there is no server, no upload, no cloud processing.

Why convert M4A to MP3?

While M4A offers better compression than MP3, some older devices, car stereos, and media players don't support it. MP3 is universally compatible with virtually every device and application.

Can I convert iPhone voice memos?

Yes! iPhone voice memos are saved as M4A files. Simply transfer the file to your computer (via AirDrop, email, or Files app) and upload it here.
