Privacy policy

UPDATED 29 AUG 2026
Your files never leave your device.Everything below is detail. That sentence is the policy.
01

Files you process

The files you select stay on your device. They are read into browser memory, processed locally, and offered back as a download. At no point are they transmitted. Close the tab and the memory is released.

02

Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_<id>, which hold a random identifier for two years so returning visits are not double-counted. They carry nothing about you or your files. Transmute sets no cookies of its own. One localStorage key, transmute-recent-outputs, remembers the name, size and tool of your last 8 downloads so the Recent Output list can show them. That is metadata only — never file contents, and it never leaves your browser. Clearing site data removes it.

03

Analytics

Privacy-respecting analytics may record aggregate traffic — page views and popular tools. No personally identifiable information is collected, and nothing about the files you process is ever recorded.

04

Advertising

Transmute carries no advertising, and no ad network is loaded on any page.

05

External resources

The heavy WebAssembly engines some tools rely on — FFmpeg.wasm and MuPDF — are served from this site rather than a third-party CDN, so opening a video or PDF tool hands nobody else your IP address. Every font is self-hosted too, including the Markdown to PDF tool's print typefaces. Beyond the analytics described above, the site makes no third-party requests at all — and nothing it fetches ever carries your file data.

06

Children's privacy

Transmute does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The site is a general-purpose utility and requires no account or personal data entry.

07

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any change is reflected on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy? Reach the maintainer at ishank.dev.
