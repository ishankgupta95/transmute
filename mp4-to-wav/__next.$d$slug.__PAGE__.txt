Editing suites, mastering chains, transcription services and speech-to-text tools generally want uncompressed PCM in a WAV container, and some of them refuse anything else outright. This tool bridges the two: it demuxes the audio stream out of the MP4, decodes it, and writes a standard 16-bit WAV at the track's own sample rate, which is the shape those tools expect to receive. When you just want something to listen to, MP3 is the better output and the audio extractor offers it.
